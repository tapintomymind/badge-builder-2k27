Body cause

Physique folds back in from the full-bleed strip; the 20 attributes become 31px single-line rows — 2,076px of pane content drops to ~950px. Nearly the whole body fits one viewport.

Catalog market

The only tall scroller, and the only place scrolling remains. Filter bar pinned; category headers sticky, carrying their own token digests. JumpNav's chips become filters — its anchors point at panels that are now simply always on screen.

The build result

What you're making, permanently visible: totals strip (entry moves behind Edit budgets…), the roster (absorbing the Loadout board), and the synergy dock pinned at the bottom — the slot is always in reach.

The lit loop — why this feels connected

Synergy assignment stops being a dropdown 6,500px below the grid and becomes a cross-column gesture: press a slot's empty Fuse in the dock → eligible purchased badges glow in the catalog (everything else dims) → click one → it snaps into the slot. Same mechanic in reverse: hovering a roster row or a slot pair flashes its cards in the catalog. The three columns aren't three pages sharing a screen — the highlight system is what makes them one machine. Category hue is the second thread: the same six accents key the attribute groups, the section headers, the totals cells, and the roster groups, so a category reads as one horizontal band across all three columns.

Component family

One card anatomy, five members

Every badge-shaped surface shares zones — identity (name · tier · category edge), status (level pips, B/S/G/H/L lettered, never color alone), meta (cost, requirement), actions. Prose appears only when it's actionable: blocked cards state their gate, everything else waits for expand.

Cost, honestly

What this retires, what it keeps

Retired or superseded

  • JumpNav — its panel anchors point at things that are now always on screen; its category chips become filters.
  • PhysiqueStrip (F13) — dissolves into the Body rail at L; the <768 carve-out becomes the Build tab.
  • Ledger overview panel + Loadout board tiles — merge into the totals strip and roster groups.
  • Budget grid as always-mounted panel — becomes the Edit budgets… drawer (the Bonus dialog's pattern).
  • F14 shell arithmetic — MIN_SHELL_H, col-right scroll memory scope, and tests/layout-arithmetic.test.ts get recut for three scrollports.
  • Section auto-collapse latch — mostly moot when nothing shares an axis with the grid.

Carried through unchanged

  • Every token — surfaces, metals, category hues, spacing, motion. This is a re-cut, not a re-skin.
  • The engine seam — every number on every new surface is still an engine readout; no rule moves into the UI.
  • H2 overlay discipline — preview toggles stay display-only; the totals strip renders the "current" basis, projections stay labelled.
  • Levels lettered, never color alone — pips keep B/S/G/H/L; stale and over states keep their stated reasons.
  • Persistence machinery — autosave, quarantine, drift disclosures, the switcher guard: untouched.
  • 44px touch floor at S; compact desktop densities never ship below it on phone.
Slice 1

The shell

Three-column grid, per-column scrollports, totals strip, roster (absorbing board + overview), synergy dock, budgets drawer. Existing components move; none redesigned yet.

Slice 2

The card family

Compact/expanded catalog card, roster row, slot chip — one anatomy, all states (owned / blocked / stale / candidate), roll controls into the expanded state.

Slice 3

The lit loop + phone

Cross-column picking and hover-tracing; then the tab bar, per-tab scroll memory, and the picking flow across tabs.
