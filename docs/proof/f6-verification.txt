== The defect, as measured ==
User report: the right rail is "a bit too skinny, and items are not laying next
to each other as smoothly."

Measured in headless Chrome at 1280 against the F5 tip, BEFORE any change:
  .rail-right                    176px
  .section content box           142px   (176 − 2 border − 2×16 padding)
  .ledger-overview content box   118px   (142 − 2×12 well padding)   <-- here
  .ledger-overview__row demand   168px   ("Rebounding" 76 + 16 gap + "0/0 · 0/—" 76)

All 6 ledger rows wrapped onto two lines — in the EMPTY state, with no build
loaded. Row height 54px against a 29px single line; the Ledger overview section
stood 450px tall to show 6 numbers.

Root cause is a seam between two commits, not a bad number in either:
  F5.0 (3a12f64) derived the right rail against a 142px content box.
  F5   (ad4d382) then styled .ledger-overview as an inset "instrument well"
       with `padding: var(--space-2) var(--space-3)` — 24px of sides.
The geometry slice had already spent that 24px. Nothing re-derived the rail,
because tests/layout-arithmetic.test.ts I8 compared RAIL_RIGHT − SECTION_CHROME
and stopped there: the well was invisible to it. I8's constant was also the
wrong QUANTITY — LEDGER_ROW_MIN = 137 is min-content ("Playmaking" + "10/16"),
the width at which the text breaks, while a flex row wraps at MAX-content. I8
would have passed at 142px even with the well counted.

This is precisely the escalation the F5 reportback named: "if any later addition
pushes the right rail's min-content above 142px [...] the answer is the §11.8 IA
re-cut, not another shave." F5's own paint slice was that addition.

== The re-cut ==
L (1280–1439): 280/176 → 258/204 · well sides --space-3 → --space-1 ·
               ledger row column gap --space-4 → --space-2
XL (≥1440), NEW: 300/268 · well sides and row gap both restored to F5's values

258 is the LEFT rail's derived floor, not a preference: 258 − 34 chrome = 224 =
the §3.1 usable slider track (I9). 258 + 204 = 462 sits ON the I3 ceiling — the
centre column is 745px at 1280 with a 17px scrollbar against a 744px 3-up
minimum. There is nothing left to take at 1280; moving either rail further now
requires moving a floor, and the arithmetic test says so in its own words.

XL exists because 1280 is the WORST case for the rails and rev 5 made every
wider viewport pay its bill — the rails stayed pinned at their 1280 sizes while
the centre absorbed all the extra width, so a 1728px display wrapped its ledger
rows for a shortfall that only existed at 1280.
