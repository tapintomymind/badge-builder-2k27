0. HOW EVERY NUMBER BELOW WAS TAKEN — no dev server
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Chrome/151, --headless=new, --force-device-scale-factor=1, driven over CDP
(Runtime.evaluate / Emulation.setDeviceMetricsOverride) against the
PRODUCTION output of `vite build --base=./`, loaded from file://. No vite dev
server was started at any point in this slice.

BEFORE and AFTER are two builds of the SAME worktree — dev @a5fe8e1 built to
a separate outDir with the slice stashed, and the slice's own dist — measured
by the same driver in the same session.

ONE MEASUREMENT TRAP, RECORDED BECAUSE IT MADE THE FIRST TWO PROBE RUNS LIE.
The app flushes its autosave on pagehide/visibilitychange. Clearing
localStorage from the app's own page and then navigating re-persists the state
you just deleted, and re-stamping the autosave's dataVersion from the app's
page gets overwritten by the flush on the way out — which is why the drift
banner "would not render" twice. Both operations are done from a neutral page
in the same file:// storage bucket instead. Every state below was re-taken
after the fix.

4. THE ARRANGEMENT — A CAP, NOT A STRETCH
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
.physique-strip__row is a 3-track grid:

  grid-template-columns: max-content max-content minmax(0, 1fr)
    col 1  the Position SegmentedControl   256.73px  AT EVERY WIDTH
    col 2  the HeightField fieldset        214.02px  (228.36 at "Any", see below)
    col 3  the Position Hint               the ONLY item allowed to grow,
                                           and `.hint`'s own 65ch caps it

MEASURED, every state, both widths, with and without the clamp notice and the
drift banner:  .number-field input  =  56px × 56px.  UNCHANGED.

The ~434px-each defect that `.summary`'s track cap and F5.4/A1 were both
written to prevent is structurally unreachable here: `width: 56px` is the base
rule, nothing in the strip re-declares it, and no track it sits in is a
percentage or an `auto`. Pinned as an absence, not an assumption — F13
assertion 2 walks every `.physique-strip .number-field` block and fails on any
`width:` in it.

CANARY, derived: hand those two inputs a `1fr` track each in the strip's
1248px content box and they resolve to 600px apiece. That is the arithmetic of
"full width", taken literally.

Two secondary measurements the arrangement bought:
  · `.number-field` is turned on its side inside the strip only
    (flex-direction: row). The stacked label-over-input idiom is correct for
    the AttributeGrid and the 12-field budget table and costs 23.2px of pure
    height in a bar whose whole job is to be one row. The stacked idiom is
    untouched everywhere else.
  · The Height <legend> is re-pointed at `.segmented legend`'s treatment for
    the strip only. `.attr-group legend` is uppercase / letter-spaced /
    --fg-muted with --space-2 below; beside "Position" it read as a different
    rank of thing and sat 4px lower. AttributeGrid's six group legends keep
    the stacked idiom.

KNOWN, MEASURED, UNSPENT: col 2 is 228.36px at "Any" and 214.02px with a
position selected, because at "Any" the range hint (`5'9"–7'4", the range this
dataset covers.`) is longer than the field row and drives the max-content, and
with a position (`SF: 6'4"–6'10"`) it is shorter. So col 3 shifts 14.34px left
on the first position pick. Fixing it means either a pinned magic minimum or a
second copy amendment; neither is worth 14px.

it is the record of a value the app changed on the user's behalf, and §3.3

7. WHAT STAYED IN THE SETUP PANEL, AND WHETHER IT STILL EARNS ITS COLLAPSE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  at L (>=1280)   ONE child Section: Badge Points & Badge Slots, + Reset
  at M/S (<1280)  TWO: Attributes (no pane below 1280) + Budgets, + Reset

THE OUTER <Section title="Build"> STAYS, and the argument is a measurement,
not symmetry. It is the surface F5.3's one-shot latch closes, and closing it
turns a 595.14px block into a 53px digest row. A collapse control that buys
542px is earning its 53 — and at M/S it has two children anyway, so removing
it at L only would split one behaviour across the 1280 seam.

THE NESTED INNER SECTION is a lever, priced and unspent: unwrapping
`Badge Points & Badge Slots` at L would save one SECTION_LEAD_Y plus a gap
(53 + 12 = 65px), and it would make `section-budget` a preference that
silently stops applying above 1280. 65px is 4.7% of the lead; the seam split
is permanent. Not taken.

THE LATCH PREDICATE FOLLOWED THE SURFACE. `hasValues` at L was
`hasBudgetValues || build.position !== undefined`; the position term is GONE.
Same §16.5 rule F5.4 wrote ("scoped to what this panel renders"), applied to
the surface F13 moved — and it fixes the surprise F5.4's own addendum flagged:
"at L, picking a position FIRES THE LATCH", collapsing the panel under the
user. Verified in the browser:

  zero state, panel open                              true
  after picking SF in the strip                       true   (was: collapsed)
  after committing 20 into the first budget field     false  (latch fired)
  digest after the latch                    "20 pts · 0 Badge Slots"

M/S is bit-identical — that branch never carried the position term.

THE DIGEST dropped height and position. Both are permanently on screen in the
strip now, so a collapsed panel is not hiding them, and reciting them there
was a fourth copy of a number the user can already read.

`section-physique` retired WITH its <Section>. The other four keys —
section-attributes, section-build-panel, section-budget,
section-ledger-overview — are untouched, so no user's collapse preference
resets. F13 assertion 5.

WHY THE <Section> WRAPPER LEFT PHYSIQUE AT ALL, given §16.9 forbids exactly
that for Attributes: §16.9's argument is the KEYBOARD BYPASS — one <summary>
keystroke takes twenty sliders out of the tab order, which is worth its 70px.
Physique is two controls and THREE tab stops (a radio group contributes one).
Measured, the Section cost SECTION_CHROME_Y = 70px of chrome around content
that lays out in ~50, and collapsing it hid the two controls the ask says to
keep in view. The bypass argument does not transfer to three tab stops.
