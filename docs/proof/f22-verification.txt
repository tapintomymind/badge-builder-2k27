up: calling location.reload() right after corrupting the key let the OUTGOING

===============================================================================
Found during proof 3 and fixed in the same slice: after "Clear just the
unreadable autosave", the next boot reads "absent", so a banner keyed on the
boot outcome would have left the preserved bytes in storage with nothing
pointing at them. The banner is keyed on the quarantine KEY'S EXISTENCE
